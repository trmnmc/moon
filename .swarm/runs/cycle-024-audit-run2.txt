Predicate under test -- stated in plain words, with every number pulled
from the parsed source in Part 1, not assumed:
  Ramp: 4 glyphs, strictly increasing fill-density, topping out
        at density 1.0 (read off SHADE[-1] = U+2588 'FULL BLOCK').
  A same-class horizontally mirror-symmetric ('LEFT'<->'RIGHT'-named)
        pair at density 0.5 -- this is what KI-5's prose calls
        'a symmetric half-block pair'. This alone is the NARROW predicate,
        matching KI-5's literal wording.
  The FULL predicate additionally requires a same-class pair at density
        0.125 (matching HAIRLINE, U+258F 'LEFT ONE EIGHTH BLOCK') and one more distinct same-class
        handed pair (matching ROUND_LIMB's role -- a shape pair with no
        fraction-of-cell density, so only name-symmetry is checked).
  NARROW is deliberately narrower than what src/render.js needs (it only
        checks the half-density pair KI-5's prose names); FULL is what the
        renderer actually needs end to end (all three handed pairs it uses).

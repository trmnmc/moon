--- INSTRUMENT DEFECT IN MY OWN VERIFIER, named rather than buried ---------------------------
My first verification script split the archive on the first occurrence of "\n---\n\n" to strip
the header I had just added. That sequence also occurs INSIDE the journal body, so the split
matched a separator thousands of lines too late and reported:
    reconstructed : 495061 bytes  sha256 9f62152df5baf441b6afdb6b
    BYTE-IDENTICAL: false
    missing lines : 4876
That read as catastrophic data loss. It was not: the ARCHIVE was already correct (the writer's
own `endsWith(prefix)` check had returned true pre-write). The defect was in the verifier's
split marker. Corrected by anchoring on the actual header/body JUNCTION ("\n---\n# journal —
moon") rather than on a character sequence the payload is free to contain — the same class of
bug as cycle 8's .trim(), cycle 9's sentence-scope, cycle 19's line-wrap blindness and cycle
82's .swarm/ filter: MY regex narrower or looser than the text it measured. Fixing the
instrument is not weakening the gate (step 6.5): the corrected verifier is strictly stronger,
and it is what produced the byte-identical result above.
